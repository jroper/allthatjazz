This is Jupiter, and its moon Io.

* **Telescope:** Skywatcher Classic 250P 10" Dobsonian 1200 FL
* **Tracking:** Home built 2 circle segment equatorial platform
* **Camera:** ZWO ASI533MM Pro, CMOS monochrome 3008x3008 3.76μm pixels, 250 gain
* **Integration:** 10000x1ms exposures in each of red, green and blue, with 1% lucky stacking
* **Image capture:** ASICap
* **Stacking:** [AutoStakkert!](https://www.autostakkert.com/)
* **Post-processing:** [Gimp](https://www.gimp.org/)
