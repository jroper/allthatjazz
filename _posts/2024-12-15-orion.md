This is the Orion Nebula, also known as M42. It's a diffuse nebula, containing a mixture of neutral clouds of dust and gas, ionised gas regions, and reflection nebulae. It's approxmiately 1350 lightyears away.

* **Telescope:** Skywatcher Classic 250P 10" Dobsonian 1200 FL
* **Tracking:** Home built 2 circle segment equatorial platform
* **Camera:** ZWO ASI533MM Pro, CMOS monochrome 3008x3008 3.76μm pixels, 100 gain
* **Integration:** 200x10s luminance, 50x10s red, 50x10s green, 50x10s blue
* **Calibration:** 100x10s darks
* **Image capture:** ASIImg
* **Stacking:** [Siril](https://siril.org/)
* **Post-processing:** [Gimp](https://www.gimp.org/)
* **Skies:** Almost full moon.
