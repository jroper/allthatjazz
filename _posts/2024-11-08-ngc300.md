This is the Sculptor Pinwheel Galaxy, also known as NGC 300. It's a galaxy in the constellation of Sculptor, roughly 6 million lightyears away. It's a little smaller than the Milky Way.

* **Telescope:** Skywatcher Classic 250P 10" Dobsonian 1200 FL
* **Tracking:** Home built 2 circle segment equatorial platform
* **Camera:** ZWO ASI533MM Pro, CMOS monochrome 3008x3008 3.76μm pixels, 200 gain
* **Integration:** 150x10s luminance, 30x10s red, 30x10s green, 30x10s blue
* **Calibration:** 60x10s darks
* **Image capture:** [OpenLiveStacker](https://github.com/artyom-beilis/OpenLiveStacker) on Android phone.
* **Stacking:** [Siril](https://siril.org/)
* **Post-processing:** [Gimp](https://www.gimp.org/)
* **Skies:** Quarter moon, in an area that otherwise has Bortle 4 skies (Mt Stromlo)
